## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a welcoming, trustworthy, and organized digital sanctuary for students seeking long-term accommodation. The aesthetic bridges institutional credibility and residential warmth: it feels dependable enough to assure parents during lease commitments, yet modern, human, and friction-free for digital-native students.

The design movement is **Warm Minimalist Tactility**:
- Crisp whitespace and strict layout discipline ensure zero cognitive overload during stressful relocations.
- Balanced botanical tones project stability, safety, and natural comfort without relying on cold clinical corporate tropes.
- Surfaces leverage delicate hairline containment and organic botanical tinted shadows rather than exaggerated glassmorphic blurs or artificial neon effects.
- Direct, empathetic touchpoints highlight safety indicators, transparent fee structures, verified statuses, and domestic amenities.

## Colors
The palette balances grounding forest greens, fresh mint accents, warm amber calls-to-action, and mineral backgrounds:

- **Primary Green (`#176B5B`)**: Used for primary calls-to-action, key selected states, active tab indicators, and essential branding moments.
- **Primary Dark (`#0F4D42`)**: Deep emerald applied to critical hover states, prominent headers, and high-emphasis focal surfaces.
- **Mint Accent (`#55B89A`)**: Fresh supporting accent for verified checkmarks, positive feature callouts, progress tracks, and subtle icons.
- **Soft Mint Surface (`#EAF6F2`)**: Gentle tint for badge containers, highlighted card features, and secondary button backgrounds.
- **Warm Accent Tag (`#E89B4A`)**: A warm, honey-amber tone strictly dedicated to pricing callouts, promotional ribbons, "Verified Host" accolades, and urgent spot counters (e.g., "Only 2 beds left").
- **Surface Canvas (`#F8FAF8`)**: Ultra-soft tinted off-white reducing ocular fatigue during extended housing searches.
- **Surface White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure crisp card containers, search modals, and form backgrounds.
- **Primary Text (`#17221F`)**: High-contrast, deeply saturated dark pine charcoal providing superior legibility.
- **Secondary Text (`#66736E`)**: Balanced neutral sage for secondary specs, distances, and metadata.
- **Subtle Border (`#E3EAE7`)**: Delicate mineral hairlines framing inputs and dividing content tiers.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans was selected across all roles to unify clean modernist geometry with subtle humanist warmth. Its wide apertures and crisp x-height provide exceptional legibility across dense property specification matrices, price tags, and transit distances.

- **Headlines & Display:** Tightly tracked headings deliver confidence and clarity. Large titles collapse on mobile screens to avoid awkward hyphenations and maintain viewport density.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Tabular figures (`tnum`) should be enabled for pricing tables, availability metrics, and distance indicators.
- **Labels:** Microcopy, distance indicators, and badge text use medium-to-semibold weights with slight letter spacing to preserve scannability at small scales.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout system relies on an 8pt base grid implemented through a clean responsive container system:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive grid with a max width of 1280px, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, and 32px (`2rem`) screen margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Comparison matrices shift into horizontally swipeable cards.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px margins. Full-width search bar transforms into a fixed top pill with tap-to-expand sheet interactions.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** All component padding, stack gaps, and inline layouts must use strict multiples of 4px/8px via tokens `space-xs` through `space-2xl`. Section splits consistently utilize `space-2xl` to ensure calm, unhurried browsing.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation emphasizes calm spatial layering rather than dramatic artificial heights. Surfaces feel seated in natural light with subtle botanical tints:

- **Level 0 (Flat):** Base canvas (`#F8FAF8`) and flat utility containers. No shadow, partitioned only by subtle borders (`#E3EAE7`).
- **Level 1 (Card Default):** Pure white container (`#FFFFFF`) with a delicate 1px border (`#E3EAE7`) accompanied by a soft, natural ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px rgba(23, 107, 91, 0.06)`. Used on search inputs, listing cards, and room tier panels.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Card):** On user hover or drag: shadow shifts to `0px 8px 20px rgba(23, 107, 91, 0.10)`, with a subtle border tint shift to `#55B89A`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Navbars / Bottom Bars / Search Panels):** `0px 4px 24px rgba(23, 107, 91, 0.08)` combined with a 1px border (`#E3EAE7`) on resting edges.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Bottom Drawers):** `0px 16px 40px rgba(15, 77, 66, 0.16)` overlaying a neutral backdrop scrim (`rgba(23, 34, 31, 0.4)`).

## Shapes
The shape philosophy favors approachable, friendly curvature while avoiding bubbly or childish forms. 

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Amenity Chips, Badges):** 8px (`0.5rem`) to 10px radius, delivering crisp tactility.
- **Cards and Panels:** 12px (`0.75rem`) to 16px (`1rem`) (`rounded-xl`), creating structured frame windows for high-density listing photographs and amenity checklists.
- **Pills and Floating Controls:** Full pills (`9999px`) are exclusively used for map toggles, search category switches, and price badges.
- **Borders:** All structural boundaries use a consistent 1px solid hairline (`#E3EAE7`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#176B5B`, text `#FFFFFF`, corner radius 10px, padding `12px 24px`. Hover state: `#0F4D42`. Active state: slight scale down (0.98).
- **Secondary / Soft:** Background `#EAF6F2`, text `#176B5B`, corner radius 10px. Hover state: background `#DDF0E9`.
- **Outline:** Background transparent, 1px border `#E3EAE7`, text `#17221F`. Hover state: border `#176B5B`, text `#176B5B`.
- **Warm / Book Now:** Background `#E89B4A`, text `#FFFFFF`, corner radius 10px, semi-bold font. Used specifically for final conversions and immediate room holds.

### Chips & Amenity Tags
- **Amenity Chip (WiFi, AC, Food, Laundry):** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E3EAE7`, text `#17221F`, height 32px, radius 8px, padding `6px 12px`. Features a 16px icon in `#176B5B`.
- **Filter Chip (Interactive):** When active, background fills `#EAF6F2`, border transitions to `#55B89A`, text `#176B5B` with a trailing dismiss icon.

### Verified Badges
- **Nestly Verified Badge:** Background `#EAF6F2`, text `#176B5B`, 10px radius, padding `4px 8px`. Accompanied by a solid `#55B89A` shield or checkmark icon.

### Cards (Hostel & PG Listing)
- **Structure:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E3EAE7`, radius 16px, ambient shadow `0 2px 8px rgba(23, 107, 91, 0.06)`.
- **Image Header:** Aspect ratio 16:10 with overflow clip, featuring a floating verified pill in the top-left corner and a favorite heart toggle in the top-right corner.
- **Content Block:** Property title in `title-md`, secondary text indicating walking distance to college campus (e.g., "350m from North Campus"), a row of 3 core amenity chips, and a bottom row displaying the monthly rate tagged with `#E89B4A`.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Search Bar (Global Discovery):** Multi-segmented pill or rounded rectangle (12px radius). Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `#E3EAE7`, shadow level 1. Split into Location, Sharing Type (Single/Double/Triple), and Budget columns separated by 1px vertical dividers (`#E3EAE7`).
- **Standard Input:** Height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border `#E3EAE7`, radius 10px, text `#17221F`, placeholder `#66736E`. Focus state: 1.5px border `#176B5B` with an ambient glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(23, 107, 91, 0.12)`).

### Comparison Tables (Room Tiers & Amenities)
- **Table Container:** Rounded 12px with outer border `#E3EAE7`.
- **Header Row:** Surface `#F8FAF8`, text `label-md` `#66736E`, border-bottom `#E3EAE7`.
- **Row Styling:** Alternating white background with 1px borders between rows. Checkmarks in `#176B5B`, non-included items with a muted dash in `#66736E`. Hover row highlights with `#F8FAF8`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 20px box, radius 6px, border 1.5px `#E3EAE7`. Checked state: background `#176B5B`, border `#176B5B`, check icon `#FFFFFF`.
- **Radio Buttons:** 20px circle, border 1.5px `#E3EAE7`. Selected state: border `#176B5B` enclosing a solid `#176B5B` center dot.
